How it works

This is a discrete-event simulation: instead of ticking every second, it jumps from one event (a car arrives, a car finishes at a booth) to the next. Gaps between arrivals and service times are random exponential numbers. If ρ ≥ 1, cars arrive at least as fast as the booths can serve them and the line grows without bound; try 2 booths at λ=6, µ=2.5 and watch.
